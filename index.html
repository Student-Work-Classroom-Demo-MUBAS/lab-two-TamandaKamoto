Bingu wa Mutharika

The Third President of Malawi

The former President of Malawi Bingu wa Mutharika

Biography

Bingu wa Mutharika was born on 24 February 1934 and sadly passed away on 5 April 2012. He was a Malawian economist who later became a politician and served as the third president of Malawi from 2004 until his death in 2012. He was a member of the Democratic Progressive Party (DPP) where he served as the president from 2005 until his death.

During Bingu wa Mutharika's first term as president, Malawi's economy experienced steady growth, inflation fell and the poverty rate decreased. In addition to that, he introduced a fertiliser subsidy program that significantly boosted maize production and improved food security. All this won him public support especially in the rural areas such that he was re-elected president in 2009 with a landslide victory.

"Africa is not a poor continent, but the African people are poor."
— Bingu wa Mutharika

Timeline

  1. 1934 — Born in Thyolo, Nyasaland.
  2. Studied Bachelor of Commerce at the University of Delhi, India.
  3. Studied a Master's Degree in Economics at the University of Delhi, India.
  4. Studied a Doctorate in Economics at the Pacific Western University, California.
  5. Worked as a Civil Servant in Zambia and Malawi.
  6. Served as a Loans Officer at the World Bank.
  7. Acted as the Secretary General for COMESA.
  8. 2004 — Elected as the third President of Malawi.
  9. 2009 — Re-elected as the President of Malawi.
  10. Served as the eighth Chairperson of the African Union.
  11. 2012 — Passed away while in office.
