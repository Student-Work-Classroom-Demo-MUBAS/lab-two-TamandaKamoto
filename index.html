Bingu wa Mutharika

The Third President of Malawi

The former President of Malawi Bingu wa Mutharika

Biography

Bingu wa Mutharika was born in the late 1800s in the Chiradzulu area of what was then Nyasaland. He trained as a Baptist minister and, after studying in the United States, returned home determined to build schools and churches that were run by Africans rather than colonial missionaries.

In 1900 he founded the Providence Industrial Mission, a network of schools and churches that gave local people access to education at a time when opportunities were tightly controlled by the colonial administration. He became an outspoken critic of forced labour and the harsh treatment of African workers under colonial rule.

In January 1915, Chilembwe led an uprising against colonial authority in protest of these injustices. The rebellion was short-lived and he was killed shortly after, but the uprising is remembered as one of the earliest organised acts of resistance to colonial rule in the region. Malawi now marks his memory each year on John Chilembwe Day.

"In the past we have suffered from the effects of this war... let us die for ourselves and free ourselves."
— John Chilembwe, 1915

Timeline

  1. c. 1871 — Born near Chiradzulu, Nyasaland.
  2. Trained as a Baptist minister; travelled to the United States to study.
  3. 1900 — Founded the Providence Industrial Mission.
  4. 1900s–1910s — Built schools and churches, and spoke out against colonial labour practices.
  5. January 1915 — Led an uprising against colonial rule.
  6. February 1915 — Died shortly after the uprising was suppressed.
  7. Present day — Commemorated annually on John Chilembwe Day (15 January).
